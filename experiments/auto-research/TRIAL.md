# General research progress capture and sharing

Status: bounded experiment, 2 November 2026. This record defines the trial's
question, comparison and evidence limits.

## Question

Can one OmniGraph configuration preserve useful progress on a formal question
and a subjective writing question, then let a participant understand the
evidence, uncertainty and next useful contribution without the original chat?

The result is graph configuration, two recorded examples, trial evidence and a
proposal for the complete community research journey. This is not an interface,
solver, scheduler or model-training implementation.

Keep graph structure and the rules for using it independently configurable.
`progress.pg` owns the records and relations. `progress.gq` supplies the executed
queries. `usage.yaml` proposes their explicit mapping to agent operations and
wider admission, judgment, sharing and authority policy. No application adapter
loads that policy profile in this experiment. A new domain can use these roles
as data; an arbitrary new schema requires explicit remapping and verification.
Use code for deterministic checks, Jev for proposed semantic decisions and a
generative model for proposed grounded explanations. Jev was not called here.

## Design contract

A problem records its objective, constraints and current evaluation protocol.
Hypotheses are optional. Candidates and attempts have stable identities.
Evidence distinguishes computations, observations, reports, simulations and
judgments. A recorded assessment binds the problem revision, exact protocol,
evidence and assessor. Qualifying an assessment is separate from admitting its
underlying contribution. Changed contracts require fresh assessment.
Qualification compares the captured method, qualification rule, criterion
kind/role/definition/evaluator/unit with their current values. A changed label
alone is not a scientific change. Raw evidence integrity and actor trust remain
separate from these equality checks.

Next steps preserve the open question, reason, expected information and any
prerequisites. They are invitations to contribute, not permission to execute.
Participants express an interest. A progress update explains what changed, why
it matters and which contribution would help. Queries route those records by
declared interest. No push notification or automatic adaptation is claimed.

## Two examples

1. Test the claim that n*n+n+41 is prime for every nonnegative integer. A first
   finite search without a counterexample remains inconclusive. An expanded
   search and independently checkable factorization supply a counterexample.
   This is an actual bounded arithmetic computation, not frontier mathematics.
2. Shorten a sentence about sharing research progress while preserving its
   meaning. Store several rewrites, actual word counts, and an attributable
   model assessment of semantic fidelity. Human literary preference remains
   unreviewed. The shortest candidate must not become the winner by default.

These are contrasting uses of the same schema. No domain name is an enum or a
branch in stored-query logic. Future sensor, movement-model or novel-opening
research must supply its own evaluation contract and evidence as ordinary data.
This is structural extensibility, not demonstrated scientific validity in those
domains.

## Discriminating checks

- Retrieve the objective, active criteria, all attempts, evidence, assessments
  and reasoned next steps for each example.
- Preserve the initial inconclusive search after recording the counterexample.
- Admit the writing example without a hypothesis or a numeric preference score.
- Return relevant updates to an interested participant and none to an unrelated
  participant. Retain update reasons and exact evidence references.
- Change the writing evaluation contract. Earlier assessments remain in history
  but are absent from qualified-current results until reassessed.
- Reject malformed or duplicate contributions without changing the export.
- Export, recreate a fresh graph and recover equivalent query results.
- Give a fresh agent only the exported research record and queries or query
  output. Verify that it identifies the unresolved judgment and next useful
  contribution rather than inventing a winner or repeating the failed claim.

## Conditions, limits and source

Use the already selected OmniGraph 0.14.0 release, source revision
60bfd19db780fdff3a5178f959c1204143420114. The release archive is verified against
its published SHA-256. All state, outputs and processes belong to this trial.
Keep other experiments, main, installed applications and services unchanged.

The hardware-program cookbook at revision
db957f63c65e40c3f890cd05b4f706a6a3ff607d informs separate definitions/runs,
measurable criteria, review snapshots and stale verification. Company-brain
informs source-backed assessments and explicit contradictions. Their code is
not copied. The configuration is authored for this bounded question.

Cap the recorded arithmetic search at n=100. Use three writing candidates.
Allow one independent model review and one fresh-context code/record review,
with at most one corrective implementation pass. No paid model, model download,
GPU training, physical experiment, publishing, merge or service restart is
required. Stop acceptance if the proof-size cap or required review fails.
The two arithmetic scopes are staged records derived from one bounded
computation, not independent repeated experiments. The model review is a
recorded contribution replayed as input during graph proving, not a fresh model
call on each run. The proving command requires its explicit --review path.

## Evidence boundaries

The trial can establish local graph behavior, actual arithmetic/word-count
measurements and one agent's ability to read the record. It cannot establish
human agreement about prose, arbitrary-domain validity, frontier discovery,
multi-user access control, scale or automatic notification delivery. Every
remaining gap must be visible in the result and the community proposal.
