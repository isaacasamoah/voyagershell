## Completed contract

`cartographer-single-claim-v5` defines:

- durable truth as an assertion that holds beyond the utterance rather than a
  snapshot;
- the hard-core null boundary: asserted absence of a settled decision,
  conclusion, outcome, choice, or determination, plus greetings, thanks,
  acknowledgements, and whole-content promises to speak later;
- the ordered first-match type decision `preference` → `operational` →
  `domain`; and
- the rim rule: classify what the claim asserts, not what a reader may do with
  it.
